{# Two beads wrapped by a closed vesicle: the interaction energy u(s) of van der Wel et al., Sci. Rep. 6, 32825 (2016), Fig. 3e / 4a.

   Units: bead radius = 1 (D_p = 2), kappa = KB/2 = 1 energy unit (the code's bending energy is KB * H^2, H = (c1 + c2)/2),
   vesicle radius Rv = 6.25 (D_v/D_p = 6.25, as in the paper's simulation). To go to the paper's units: u [kBT] = 20.9 u [kappa],
   length [um] = 0.49 length.

   What differs from Wrapping_two_cov / Wrapping_two_fixed, and why (Studies/two_beads_vesicle/README.md):
     * floppy membrane: sigma~ = sigma a^2/kappa = 0.02 (the floppy vesicles of the experiment have sigma~ < 0.03), the old runs had
       sigma~ = 2.3 (KB 0.85) to 16 (KB 0.12): there is no bending-mediated interaction left at that tension
     * the wrapped area is fixed by an adhesive PATCH with a sharp edge ("patch_angle", the theta_Wr of the paper's Eq. 4), the contact
       line is pinned and the cap does not change with the separation; the soft shell of the Coverage energy (+-25 % of the radius)
       makes the wrapped fraction depend on the neck, and with it the energy at the level of the signal (0.1 kappa = 0.2 % of the bending)
     * frozen beads, the vesicle finds its position; the axis of each patch is fixed in space (Run_two_vesicle_chain.py points it to the
       centre of the vesicle again at every separation). "patch_free": true (generator: --free_axis) lets it turn, the axis being the
       one that maximises the covered weight at every evaluation, every solve starting from the axis set for the link (it finds the
       maximum within about 25 degrees of it). Needs the build with commit 8ced2182 of the branch bead-patch-adhesion: in the merge 68af27c6
       the solve started from where the last one ended and was dragged onto a false maximum (+2 kappa, a closed neck). Not the default: it
       gains 0 to 0.01 kappa at 98 percent and at 90 percent 0.1 to 0.4, inside the scatter between relaxations (README, checks of the patch)
   wrap_mode: "patch" (default: Adhesion with the angular patch, needs the build with patch_angle), "coverage" (the Coverage energy
   as in Wrapping_two_cov), "adhesion" (plain Adhesion, the wrapping is whatever the energy gives)
   area_mode: "tension" (default: Surface_tension sigma, no area constraint), "constraint" (Area_constraint, the paper's A_t = (1 + excess) A_sphere
   with a fixed volume), "excess" (Excess_tension: free area until A_t, then a tension), "none"
   bending: "Bending" (default) or "Bending_tan" (generator: --bending): the same energy sum KB H_v^2 A_v with H_v = (1/4) sum_e l_e t_e / A_v, the edge term
   t_e being the dihedral angle theta_e for "Bending" and 2 tan(theta_e / 2) for "Bending_tan" (every run before 2026-10-12), so kappa = KB/2 for both.
   They differ by a factor 1 + theta^2/12 per edge (1.4 percent at the largest angle of these meshes, 0.4 rad; the tan version diverges for a fold, theta -> pi,
   which these meshes do not reach): on the stored planar chains the two energies of the same shape differ by 0.09 kappa (98 percent) and 0.08 kappa (90 percent),
   constant in s to 0.01 kappa, so the switch is not expected to change u(s)
   outside: 1 (default) the beads are outside the vesicle, -1 inside (needs the build with "outside": -1 for Adhesion; the Coverage energy does not know it)
   planar: true (generator: --planar) a flat disk with a clamped edge (input/Big_planar_mem.obj, radius 10 * rescale, normal along x) instead of the closed vesicle:
   no volume constraint, "boundary": true, the beads above the disk (x > 0) pushing into it; the large-vesicle limit of the experiment (D_v/D_p >= 14) #}
