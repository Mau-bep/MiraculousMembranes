{# One bead wrapped by a closed vesicle (or by a flat disk): the starting state of a chain that scans one control parameter
   (patch angle, wrapped fraction, adhesion, tension, excess area, volume or Coverage target), Studies/one_bead_vesicle/README.md.
   Written by Create_subjob_one_vesicle.py, walked by Run_one_vesicle_chain.py. The sister of Wrapping_two_vesicle.txt.

   Units: bead radius a = 1, kappa = KB/2 = 1 energy unit (the code's bending energy is KB * H^2, H = (c1 + c2)/2), vesicle radius Rv = 6.25 a
   (the paper's D_v/D_p). Energies come out in kappa: E [kBT] = 20.9 E [kappa] for the kappa of the paper, length [um] = 0.49 length.

   wrap_mode: "frenkel" (default: the Frenkel_Normal_nopush bead-membrane interaction of the earlier wrapping studies, cut at "rc" (absolute length,
   1.2 a); no patch, the contact line is free, the wrapped fraction is whatever bending, tension and adhesion give), "adhesion" (the Adhesion
   interaction with its cosine shell, no patch, free contact line), "patch" (Adhesion limited to an angular patch with a sharp edge, the theta_Wr of
   van der Wel et al., pinned contact line; needs the build with patch_angle), "coverage" (the Coverage energy: the wrapped fraction is a target)
   area_mode: "tension" (default: Surface_tension sigma, free area: the floppy vesicle), "constraint" (Area_constraint, A_t = (1 + excess) A_sphere
   with a fixed volume: the paper's ensemble, the tension is then the multiplier of the constraint), "excess" (Excess_tension: free area until A_t,
   then a tension), "none"
   bending: "Bending" (default) or "Bending_tan": the same energy sum KB H_v^2 A_v, the edge term being the dihedral angle or 2 tan(angle/2)
   outside: 1 (default) the bead is outside the vesicle, the membrane invaginates around it; -1 inside (needs the build with "outside": -1 for Adhesion)
   planar: true a flat disk with a clamped edge (input/Big_planar_mem.obj, radius 10 * rescale, normal along x) instead of the closed vesicle: no volume
   constraint, "boundary": true, the bead above the disk (x > 0) pushing into it
   "patch_free": true lets the patch turn (the axis maximises the covered weight at every evaluation, every solve starting from the axis of the link; needs the
   build with commit 8ced2182 of the branch bead-patch-adhesion). The numbers of "stopping" end each run (a window of steps with a net energy change below
   tol * |E| for `patience` windows in a row), "timesteps" is only the cap #}
